# Section E, wave 1 — plan-only with-skill arms (2026-08-25)

First with-skill runs of the project. Condition `with_skill_v1` per the pre-registered
`evals/withskill_protocol.md`; six fresh reps (S0 ×3, D0 ×3) on pristine fixture copies;
captures in `results/proposals_e/`, tabulation in `results/proposals_e/WAVE_RECORD_E1.json`.

## Headline: both pre-registered criteria PASS at 3/3, against 0/3 baselines

| Arm | Baseline | Bar (criterion G, frozen v2.1) | With-skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S0** register recognition | **0/3** proposed a realtime graphical system; 2/3 argued against WebGL | ≥ 2/3 classify into S + propose a realtime/graphical system | **3/3** — every rep names the S register explicitly and weighs Q/W/S in writing |
| **D0** unprompted catalog discovery | **0/3** searched; all hand-rolled | ≥ 2/3 search AND meet D1 decision-quality | **3/3** — every rep ran a live, cited catalog sweep and produced D1-grade verdicts |

Quote-level evidence per rep is in the wave record; captures carry the full proposals.
These are the two gaps the skill's lines 3 and 4 were written from — both moved from floor
to ceiling on first contact, on the exact de-leaded prompts that produced the baseline zeros.

## Secondary findings (unscored, all on the record)

1. **Unprompted transfer of lines 1–2.** The D0 brief never mentions pausing or
   verification, yet all three D0 reps designed offscreen/hidden pause plumbing and
   real-condition verification matrices into their plans — one rep even reasoned that
   flag-assertion is required because Chromium's own hidden-tab throttling would mask a
   missing handler (the exact rationale behind the harness's M2b instrument).
2. **No spectacle inflation, no component soup.** On D0's "subtle" brief, 3/3 chose W and
   declined S in writing — the skill did not push expressiveness where it wasn't wanted
   (relevant to criterion F's pairing risk). Catalog awareness produced zero adoption
   sprees: wholesale drop-ins were declined twice on dependency-coherence grounds, and one
   rep found a real bug in the very component it recommends adapting.
3. **Premise convergence is untouched.** All three S0 reps again landed in the
   "Meridian file / you're holding the playhead" concept family (now 6/6 across baseline +
   with-skill S0). The skill moves register decisions, not premise diversity — and all
   three independently declined spatial-3D spectacle. The owner's "pinnacle" question
   (3D worlds/depth) therefore remains squarely with the `landing_love_calibration.md`
   ablation, unconfounded by this wave.
4. **Freshness line visibly working.** One rep corrected its own training-data belief in
   writing (motion v12 → verified v13.1.1); another re-verified relayed research
   first-hand before relying on it and labeled the remainder "agent-reported".
5. **Cost note for criterion D.** With-skill discovery is materially heavier: reps
   commissioned live web-research children (~70–100k tokens each). Real research is a
   real cost of skill line 3; token overhead accounting binds in the implementation arms.

## Deviations (documented, none content-bearing)

Four of six reps delegated research to background children and stalled on a
notification-routing quirk (child completions woke the orchestrator, not the parent).
Each parent was resumed with its own commissioned report relayed **verbatim** plus an
explicit mismatch escape hatch; all four confirmed the research as theirs. The two D0
sweeps were near-identical in scope, so their parent assignment is by completion order and
not provably correct — materially equivalent, both accepted, recorded honestly. Full list
in the wave record, including the standing environment confounds.

## What this does and does not establish

Established: the two plan-only skill lines produce their intended behavior change at the
Fable tier, at 3/3 strength, under pre-registered criteria and prompts.
Not established: implementation-layer effects (M2b pass rates with-skill on I2/I5, S1
quality under blind ranking, register-fit preservation on Q1/W1, token overhead ceilings) —
those are the remaining Section E arms, all still gated on the owner's go.
