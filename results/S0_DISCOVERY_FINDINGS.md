# S0 + discovery baselines (2026-08-20)

Fifteen plan-only runs per evals/spectacle_evals.json (S0) and evals/discovery_evals.json
(D0/D1/D2 + D2 no-catalog controls), Fable, fresh contexts. Proposals + wave record in
results/proposals21/. The wave survived two org spend-limit interruptions and one machine sleep;
failed-state agents resumed from saved transcripts, stopped-state agents relaunched on pristine
copies, and no partial artifact was ever counted.

## S0 - register recognition: the spectacle register is never self-selected (0/3)

Given "unforgettable, experimental, full license" and no technology named, all three reps produced
excellent top-of-W concepts - and all three converged on the same mechanism family (the page as a
playable Meridian file: scrub timelines, live curve editors, export-your-own-motion). None proposed
a visually dominant realtime/generative/spatial system; two explicitly argued AGAINST WebGL as
brand-aligned ("the product sells dependency-free CSS/JS"). That argument is defensible design
reasoning - which is exactly why the finding matters: the S lane is not even being weighed. The
skill's activation content gains its evidence-backed line: when a brief signals
unforgettable/experimental, explicitly consider the spectacle register and choose knowingly.

## Layer D - awareness is the entire gap

- **D0 (0/3 search):** unprompted, no rep ever looked for existing primitives - all hand-rolled
  contour fields with genuinely rigorous npm/license diligence (ShaderToy rejected over
  CC BY-NC-SA; a CC-BY asset avoided over attribution).
- **D1 (3/3 unanimous):** with the registry visible, all three independently reached the same
  evidence-based verdicts - adapt React Bits Topography (identity re-authored to site tokens),
  adopt Motion Primitives Magnetic (tuned) - reading component source in full, applying license
  gates (plain-MIT preferred over MIT+Commons-Clause), and rejecting a Matter.js candidate as
  dependency-incoherent.
- **D2 vs controls (3/3 vs 3/3):** with catalogs in view and a bespoke-identity brief, every rep
  swept the catalogs, found nothing on-material, classed near-misses reference-only, and routed
  custom on substrates - zero derivative drift versus the no-catalog controls. One rep rejected
  found primitives precisely because they were "recognizable, hence under-authored as identity."

Criterion G baseline readings: S0 gap confirmed; D0 gap confirmed; D1 decision quality at ceiling;
D2 abundance resistance passes. The with-skill bar for D0 (search in >=2/3) and S0 (recognize in
>=2/3) is now meaningfully above baseline (both 0/3).

## Observation for future blind rankings

Between-run concept diversity is low: all six D2 arms landed in one "the name signs itself" ink
family, and all three S0s in the Meridian-file family. Distinctiveness judgments between reps of
the same condition partly measure execution variation, not concept variation.

## Skill v1 content, as evidenced so far (four lines)

1. Ambient/continuous animation must pause offscreen/hidden - unprompted - and be verified by
   measurement (I2: 0/3 unprompted; I5: 1/3 even when asked).
2. Verify behavioral claims under the actual condition, not a proxy (I5 rep3's document.hidden
   "verification").
3. Before hand-building a common visual effect, check the component catalogs/registry; then apply
   the fit policy (D0 0/3 vs D1 3/3-at-ceiling).
4. When the brief signals unforgettable/experimental, explicitly weigh the spectacle register and
   choose it knowingly or decline it explicitly (S0 0/3).

Everything else measured across 60+ runs remains baseline-native. S1 implementation baseline is
the remaining open block.
