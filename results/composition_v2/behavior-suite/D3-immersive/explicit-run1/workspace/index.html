Observer’s guide

Make a little space
for the sky.

  1. Follow the passage.

    Move the passage slider, or choose Arrival, Alignment, and Departure. The moon’s position, distance, and trail change with you.

  2. Steer your telescope.

    Drag horizontally across the sky or use Telescope bearing. At Alignment, choose Center to match the station’s reference line.

  3. Set your own pace.

    Nothing advances without you. Turn motion off for immediate scene changes. Your device’s reduced-motion setting is respected automatically.

Using a keyboard? Tab to a slider and use the arrow keys. Every stage also has its own button.
