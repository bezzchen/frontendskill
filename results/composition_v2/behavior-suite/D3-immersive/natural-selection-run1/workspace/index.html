A note from the observatory

Nothing stays.
That’s why we look.

Iona is an imaginary moon with no planet to call home. For a moment, its long migration brings it into our field of view. This observatory is a place to notice that moment.

There’s no right speed and no perfect angle. Move through its passage, find your own alignment, and watch it leave.

Observer’s handbook

Make a little space
for wonder.

There is no task to finish. You control how Iona crosses this imaginary sky.

Follow the passage
Drag the passage slider or choose Arrival, Alignment, or Departure. With a keyboard, focus a slider and use the arrow keys.
Find your alignment
Shift Your viewpoint to move Iona against the fixed guide stars. At the middle of its passage and 0°, the moon meets the station’s reference line.
Let it wander
Play the passage, or pause it wherever you like. Playback pauses when you leave the sky or switch tabs. With reduced motion, this control becomes “Next chapter” and moves directly to the next stage without animation.

All celestial names, distances, and readings in this observatory are imagined.
